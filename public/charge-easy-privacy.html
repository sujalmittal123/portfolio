Core Privacy Guarantee: Charge Easy is an offline-first battery analytics tool. The app does not request or possess the Android Internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET). It has zero capability to transmit your device data over the network, contains no ads or analytics trackers, and stores all readings exclusively inside your device's local sandbox.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

Charge Easy does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers. The application only accesses standard on-device battery telemetry via Android's local BatteryManager API:

  • Instantaneous current (CURRENT_NOW in mA)
  • Battery voltage and temperature in °C
  • Battery level percentage and charging status
  • Battery manufacturer technology and design capacity

2. Zero Network Access Guarantee

The Android manifest of Charge Easy intentionally omits android.permission.INTERNET. The Android OS prevents the app from creating any network sockets or communicating with external servers.

No device data, battery statistics, or usage logs ever leave your smartphone.

3. Local Storage & Sandboxing

All recorded charging sessions and telemetry samples are stored exclusively in your device's private SQLite database (chargetracker_db.sqlite). This database is isolated from all other apps by Android's application sandbox.

Data is never synchronized to cloud services unless you use standard Android operating system-level device backups.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

Below is a complete explanation of every Android permission utilized by Charge Easy in compliance with Google Play Developer Policy:

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & DATA_SYNC
Allows the background charging service to log telemetry at 5-second intervals while connected to power, even when the screen is locked.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK
Maintains a partial CPU wake lock solely during active charging sessions to prevent kernel sleep from interrupting wattage logging.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Detects if the device restarts while connected to a charger, resuming tracking seamlessly.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Displays the ongoing charging notification and triggers alerts when user-configured charge limits (e.g. 80%) are reached.
android.permission.VIBRATE
Provides haptic feedback for charging completion and anti-theft disconnection alarms.
android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Ensures exact timing for disconnection guard alarms and scheduled reminders.

5. Third-Party Services & Trackers

Charge Easy contains zero third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, or advertising networks.

  • No Firebase or Google Analytics
  • No AdMob or marketing SDKs
  • No third-party crash reporters

6. Data Retention & User Control

Users maintain complete control over their recorded history:

  • You can delete individual sessions or clear the entire database inside the app settings.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored databases and logs from your device storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Charge Easy does not address anyone under 13 years of age. We do not collect personal information from children or any other users.
